# 🧠 LifeReplay AI

> **Your AI-Powered Second Brain**

LifeReplay AI is an intelligent personal knowledge management platform designed to organize every aspect of your digital life into **Intelligent Spaces**. Instead of searching through countless folders, filenames, or screenshots, users can interact with their information naturally and let AI understand the context behind it.

The vision is simple:

> **Don't remember where you saved it. Remember what it was.**

---

## ✨ Vision

Modern digital life is fragmented across PDFs, documents, presentations, screenshots, notes, photos, ZIP files, and countless other resources.

LifeReplay AI brings everything together into one intelligent workspace where users can organize, explore, and eventually interact with their memories using AI.

---

## 🚀 Current Status

**Project Stage:** Active Development 🚧

The current version focuses on building a scalable frontend architecture and a premium user experience that will serve as the foundation for future AI capabilities.

---

## 🌟 Core Concept

LifeReplay AI is built around **Intelligent Spaces**.

A **Space** represents one area of your life, such as:

* 🎓 Study
* 💼 Career
* 🚀 Startup / Hackathon
* 📸 Personal
* 🧪 Research
* 🎨 Creator

Each Space is designed to become a dedicated workspace containing documents, notes, timelines, insights, and AI assistance.
